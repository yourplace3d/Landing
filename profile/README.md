## 📍 What it is

```yaml
name:       Your Place
what:       the place around any address, as a 3D model
made_from:  open map data, real terrain, every building
runs:       in your browser, on a phone too
costs:      nothing, and it needs no account
private:    no cookies; files you load stay on your device
speaks:     [English, Deutsch, Español, Português]
licence:    MIT
```

## 🤝 Using it for work

**May I use what I make?** Yes, privately and for work. Everything is built from open map data. Keep the credit wherever you show it: "Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors".

**Print shop, school or company?** If Your Place is nearly what you need but not quite, say what is missing: [yourplace3d@gmail.com](mailto:yourplace3d@gmail.com).

## 💬 Printed one? Missing something?

A lot of what is in Your Place started as someone's message. Photos of prints and ideas for what is missing are welcome: [open an issue →](https://github.com/yourplace3d/3d-city/issues/new) or write to [yourplace3d@gmail.com](mailto:yourplace3d@gmail.com).
